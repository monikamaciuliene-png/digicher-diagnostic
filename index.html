About this instrument

The DIGICHer monitoring instrument helps cultural heritage institutions assess how inclusively and equitably they conduct digitisation of minority cultural heritage. It covers the full digitisation process: from resourcing and community participation, through technology and access, to cultural integrity and ethical governance.

The 20 indicators are derived from an evidence base spanning socio-economic criteria (D3.1), legal and ethical criteria (WP2), technology criteria (WP4), and stakeholder analysis (WP1). The full criteria derivation and Theory of Change are documented in D4.4. The instrument uses a maturity-based approach aligned with the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance.

This validation session

PART 1: Test the instrument. Choose a digitisation initiative you are familiar with and assess it using 20 indicators grouped in 6 dimensions, plus 1 cross-cutting CARE Principles indicator. For each, read the three descriptions and click the one that best matches your situation.

PART 2: Give feedback. If any indicator is unclear or needs rewording, click the Flag button and add a note. After the assessment, complete the Feedback tab.

All data is anonymous. You can save results as PDF or download as JSON from the Results tab.
